# Mechanic ideas, parked for later

Collected 2026-11-26 when dig & build left Lumenby (it played badly). Lumenby got **Relight the Sign**
(`docs/games/neon-shrine-sign.md`); the rest wait here. Each names a game that did it and what it could reuse.
Sources are the pages the research found; most are fan wikis or reviews.

## For Lumenby and the Underglow (not yet built)

- **Lantern and lightroots** (the Underglow): dark floors, a lumen meter that drains down there; throw lumen
  shards for a short light, relight fixed lamps for good (map reveal, heal, respawn). Tears of the Kingdom's
  Depths ([Lightroot](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Lightroot)), SteamWorld Dig's lantern
  ([review](https://gameinformer.com/games/steamworld_dig/b/pc/archive/2013/12/06/game-informer-steamworld-dig-review)).
  Reuses the day/night light. Risk: a dark screen on a phone outdoors; keep outlines.
- **Minecarts**: ride carts through the benches and the drift, turn rail switches with the sword or the hook.
  Oracle of Ages ([Rail Switch](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Rail_Switch)), DKC's
  [Mine Cart Carnage](https://mariowiki.com/Mine_Cart_Carnage). The carts already stand in Lumenby as props.
  Risk: deaths at speed on a small screen; warn before danger.
- **Kiln and glassblowing**: a two-input timing game at the sign-bender's kiln that makes lenses, bottles, tube
  pieces. Spiritfarer's foundry ([guide](https://twinfinite.net/guides/spiritfarer-get-glass-how-to/)).
- **Metal detector**: the detector kid's beeper as an item, beeping faster near fixed soft spots (no terrain
  editing). Skyward Sword's [dowsing](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Dowsing), Link's Awakening's
  [Seashell Sensor](https://zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Seashell_Sensor). Cheap; keep it optional.
- **Echo pulse** (the echo ledges, the glass sea): strike a tuning fork to show hidden ledges for a moment; too
  many pulses wake something. [Perception](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Perception_(video_game)).
  Must read without sound.
- **Cave-in**: a placed stone drops when what holds it goes; same-colour ore pops in chains under an air meter.
  [Dig Dug](https://digdug.fandom.com/wiki/Rock), [Mr. Driller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Driller).
- **Filling the chalk lots**: bring glass, lumen and residents met in other lands; houses go up. BotW's Tarrey
  Town ([guide](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/zelda-breath-of-the-wild-tarrey-town-quest)). Every house
  needs art.
- Set aside for overlap: a magnet glove (the hook, Luna's psi blocks), a colour lens
  ([Hue](https://pushsquare.com/reviews/ps4/hue); Yule Fjord's beams), a two-layer burrow
  ([Mole Mania](https://www.hardcoregaming101.net/mole-mania/); every room drawn twice).

## For the other lands, or new ones

- **The fossegrim's fiddle**: an offering at a waterfall on a Thursday evening teaches tunes that turn the
  weather or the hour, or wake Seter Fell's sleeping trolls. [Fossegrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fossegrim);
  Ocarina of Time's songs. Cheap (day/night exists); tunes on A, B and the directions for touch.
- **Vardøger**: record a few seconds; your double plays them ahead of you to hold switches.
  [Braid](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Braid_(video_game))'s shadow. The fixed-step input makes it nearly free.
- **Huldra sight**: a true sight that shows hidden-folk paths and the cow's tail on a villager who is huldra.
  The [Lens of Truth](https://zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Lens_of_Truth).
- **Åsgårdsreia**: on Yule night the ride sweeps Yule Fjord on a timer; hide in doorways marked with a cross or
  under the hay. Phantom Hourglass's [safe zones](https://zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Safe_Zone). Checkpoints
  generous.
- **Night radio and the aurora**: tune a pocket radio to open gates and find ghosts (Rerun Estates, the
  Underglow's broadcast layer). [Oxenfree](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/oxenfree/getting-started/the-radio-mechanic),
  Outer Wilds' [Signalscope](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/outer-wilds/walkthrough/signalscope-frequencies-uses),
  Never Alone's aurora spirits ([review](https://kotaku.com/never-alone-the-kotaku-review-1659789150)).
  Needs a visible waveform.
- **Draug wrecks**: visit drowned fishermen's last moments and name each fate so they rest.
  [Return of the Obra Dinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn). Text-heavy on a phone.
- **Tusser under the hill**: shrink at barrow stones into the hidden folk's world inside Seter Fell. Minish
  Cap's [portals](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Minish_Portal). Expensive: art at two scales.
