# Another Shore — audit of the live version (commit 1d2ad14, 2026-09-06)

Screenshots: ~/Pictures/aw/cur-*.png (1440×900 idle at 0/8/20 s, play, jump, paused) and ~/Pictures/aw-phone.png (375×667), ~/Pictures/aw-land.png (667×375).

## What works
- Physics is solid: 10 engine tests pass, coyote time + jump buffer feel right, swept collision, deterministic. Keep engine.ts and its tests.
- Input shell is careful: pointer-captured touch buttons, blur/visibility pause, navigation lock, no key-repeat toggling, reduced-motion path. No console errors.
- Palette is close to the brief (petrol sky, pale moon, coral shirt) and the one-scene-one-palette idea is right.
- Profile text unboxed upper-left over the scene is the right idea for a landing page.

## What is wrong (composition)
1. **The far layer reads as reeds/grass, not eroded towers.** Thin tapering needles with a fringe of leaf-like notches. Nothing has mass. Another World's backgrounds are big slabs, arches, monoliths — few, large, angular.
2. **Bottom ~30 % of the screen is dead dark ground.** Camera pins the ground line at 72 % height, and everything below is one flat fill plus faceted blobs. On the phone it is half the screen. The original keeps the ground as a thin plane and fills the frame with sky and mid-distance.
3. **Scale is absent.** There is nothing near the player that is bigger than the player. No foreground silhouette (the black rock/plant edge that frames Another World shots) except small dark humps at the very bottom.
4. **Sea reads as striped platforms.** Four horizontal bars at fixed y look like a ledge. The moon reflection is three bars, fine idea, but it sits on top of the "stripes" so it merges.
5. **Platforms are rectangles with pale corner ticks.** The ticks are a gameplay crutch that says "this is a collider". Ground should be shapes; edges should read from silhouette against the sea/sky, not from ticks.
6. **Hazards are 16 px spike rows** that look like rendering glitches. Deaths are instant respawn: no death animation, no beat, no reason. Another World's deaths are the storytelling.
7. **Moon** is a flat 28-gon, screen-fixed, and on the phone it overlaps the spires and the profile. Fine as a shape; wrong as the largest thing on screen when nothing else is large.
8. **Character:** head too large for the body, legs are stroked lines with a boot wedge; stiff. Readable at a glance though. Idle "bob" is ok.
9. **HUD boxes** ("BEACON 0 / 3", PAUSE, EXIT in bordered boxes) are generic web-game chrome. The original has no HUD at all.
10. **Landscape phone**: the start button wraps to two lines and overlaps the scene; profile column too wide.
11. **Level** is a corridor of gaps + spikes with one raised ledge. There is no *place* — no arch to walk under, no cliff to descend, no tower to climb, nothing that makes section 2 feel different from section 1. Scrolling parallax replaces the fixed-screen "shot" that gives the original its cinema.

## What to keep as contract for both agents
- `engine.ts` API (`createWorld/stepWorld/demoInput`) and `types.ts` are fine and tested; the fixer keeps them, the fresh build may replace them but must keep a node test file that CI runs.
- Site rules from `.claude/skills/phareim-theme/SKILL.md`: nothing scrolls, fits 375×667 and 667×375, `--theme-*` tokens on `.<id>-page`, navigation lock while playing, start on tap not touchstart, 180 ms theme fade, no chrome animation.
- Fonts: `--font-person` (Space Grotesk 300) for the person, `--font-machine` (Space Mono) for machine text.
