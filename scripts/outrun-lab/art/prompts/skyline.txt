The subject is the skyline of Neo Boston seen from a road leading out of town at nightfall,
in a 1989 that never ended: a dense row of old brick and stone towers with stepped tops,
water tanks, radio masts and smoke stacks, hundreds of small lit windows in warm sodium
yellow, and on two of the towers a huge cathode-ray television screen glowing pale blue (the
blue blocks). The walls are deep blue and brown, never black; the lit windows are flat
squares of yellow at least two blocks wide.
