The subject is a far range of high granite mountains in New England seen across the land at
blue hour, just before dawn: steep rock faces in three or four flat tones of slate blue and
violet, snow on the summits and in the gullies (the pale blocks) in cold blue-white with a
first touch of rose on the faces that look east, dark pine forest climbing the lower
slopes, a ridge line with a strong readable silhouette. No buildings, no lights. Shade in
large flat facets with hard edges, the way mountains are painted in a late-1990s arcade
