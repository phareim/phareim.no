A background painting for a late-1990s arcade fighting game, painted the way cel and gouache
backgrounds were: flat areas of solid colour with hard clean edges and dark outlines, a small
limited palette, three or four tones to a material, dense drawn detail, every surface with
wear and a story (rivets, stickers, dents, cables, grime where hands touch), strong readable
silhouettes, light with a clear direction from the upper left, shadows leaning blue-violet
and lights leaning warm. Sharp focus everywhere, no texture noise, no brush strokes.
