The first image is a layout: each coloured block on the magenta background marks the place
and the size of one thing. The second image shows the pixel-art style to draw in and the
size of square pixel block to use. Draw one thing in each block, filling its block and no
larger, each one separate with flat magenta all round it: nothing touching, no ground under
it, no shadows on the ground, no labels, no numbers, no frames, no people. Each thing is a
roadside sprite for a 1997 arcade road-racing game of the sprite-scaling kind, set at night
in a 1989 that never ended: it stands upright, is seen straight on from the road with no
perspective, and ends in a straight line at its foot.
