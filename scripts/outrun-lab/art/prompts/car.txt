The subject is the player's car in a 1997 arcade road-racing game of the sprite-scaling kind,
seen from directly behind and a little above, driving away from the viewer: a low, wide, red
1980s sports convertible with the top down. It is one sprite: the whole car and the two
people in it, nothing else.
The red block is the rear of the car's body: wide tail lamps in a dark panel, a chrome
bumper (the grey strip), a yellow number plate reading NOVA 89, twin exhaust pipes, a worn
pizza-shop sticker; the two black blocks are the rear tyres under its corners. On the boot
lid a small luggage rack with a strapped canvas duffel bag and a flat pizza box. The dark
block is the open cockpit with two low seat backs; the blue-grey block is the windscreen
frame seen beyond them.
Two people sit in it, drawn from behind, their heads and shoulders well above the seat
backs. They are the same two people who sit with their backs to the viewer in the second
image, drawn the way that image draws them. The blonde block is the driver, on the left:
