They are small decals and pop-off heads for a 1997 arcade space-combat game, each a bold,
simple icon of very few large pixels that reads at a glance, with a dark outline, lit from
the upper left, filling its block. Ten things in two rows.
Top row, left to right, five mascot faces seen straight from the front, flat and
symmetrical, like a decal painted on a fighter's nose: the face alone, no neck, no body,
filling its square. 1. a rat: grey fur, two big round tan ears, a long snout, a dark brown
nose, two white buck teeth, a red sweatband across the forehead. 2. a bear: brown fur, two
small round ears, a cream muzzle, a black nose, a black bowler hat. 3. a gorilla: dark
grey-brown fur, a heavy brow, a pale grey face, a red bow tie under the chin. 4. a hound:
tan fur, two long droopy dark brown ears hanging down the sides, a black nose, round black
sunglasses. 5. a lion: a golden face in a big round orange-gold mane, a small gold crown on
top.
Bottom row, left to right: 6. the logo of a small pizza shop: one slice of pepperoni pizza,
point down, yellow cheese, red pepperoni discs, a brown crust along the top with a round
bite taken out of one corner; no lettering. 7 to 10: the heads of the same four mascots
(the rat, the bear, the gorilla, the hound), each popped off its robot body: the head seen
from the front and tilted a little, its jaw dropped open as if singing, sitting on top of a
silver-grey coiled steel spring of three or four coils that hangs below it, a short stub of
torn red and yellow wire at the spring's lower end.
No pink anywhere: the rat's ears are tan and its nose dark brown.
