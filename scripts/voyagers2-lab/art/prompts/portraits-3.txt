Three robot mascots, one to a row, the same mascot in all three squares of its row, each a
head-and-shoulders talk portrait like the people in the second image, its glass eyes and
hinged jaw large and readable. Each wears an old brown leather flight helmet with round
flying goggles on the brow and the collar of a flight jacket.
Top row, a GORILLA pilot: a worn animatronic gorilla, dark grey-brown fabric fur with
stitched seams and a rubbed patch on the crown, a heavy brow of moulded rubber over small
round glass eyes, a broad pale grey face with flared nostrils, a big lower jaw on a hinge
with a bolt at each corner, a red bow tie at its throat over the jacket's collar, the flight
helmet stretched over its big head. Left: singing, jaw dropped wide open. Middle: calm, a
slow proud look. Right: angry, teeth bared, brow down.
Middle row, a HOUND pilot: a worn animatronic basset hound, tan and white fabric fur with
stitched seams, two long droopy dark brown ears hanging out from under the flight helmet,
a big black rubber nose, droopy jowls on a hinged jaw, round black sunglasses over its glass
eyes (the goggles pushed up above them). Cool and lazy. Left: singing, jaw dropped open.
Middle: calm, a lazy smile. Right: angry, lip curled.
Bottom row, a BEAR pilot again, drawn differently from any famous mascot bear: a worn
animatronic bear of faded honey-gold fabric fur with stitched seams and a patched ear, small
round ears, a pale cream muzzle with a big dark brown nose, small round black glass eyes, a
hinged jaw with a bolt at each corner, a battered black bowler hat worn on top of the flight
helmet, and a striped red-and-white scarf at its neck. Left: singing, jaw dropped open.
Middle: calm, a dopey smile. Right: angry, brows down.
