The enemy's pilots are animatronic mascots: the worn singing band robots of a chain of pizza
restaurants, now flying fighters. Each is a robot in a costume: fur made of fabric panels
with stitched seams and a few rubbed bald patches, round glass eyes with a painted iris and a
hard shine of one or two pale pixels, a mouth on a hinge (a lower jaw that drops on two
visible bolts), rivets and panel lines on the face, scuffed paint. Funny and a little
shabby, never scary, never gory. Every mascot is an original character invented for this
game: it resembles no real restaurant chain's mascot, no real animatronic and no character
of a video game or a film.
