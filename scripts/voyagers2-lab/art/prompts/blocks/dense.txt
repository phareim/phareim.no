The reference image is a packed sprite sheet from a 1997 arcade game, in square pixel blocks
on a flat magenta background: rows of very small sprites, the same few things repeated in
every row. Redraw it as finished pixel art and keep it exactly: every sprite in its place
and at exactly the size it has there, none enlarged, none merged with its neighbour, the same
number of rows and columns, the same size of square pixel blocks on the same grid, flat
magenta between them. One block of the reference is one pixel of the finished sprite: never
draw a detail smaller than a block, never subdivide a block. Each sprite is drawn again
cleanly as a bold shape of few pixels with a dark outline one block thick, two or three
tones of each colour, so that it reads at a glance; tidy what is ragged, doubled or muddy in
the reference. The copies of a thing may differ a little.
