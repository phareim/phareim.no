They are one gang of enemies in a 1997 vertical-scrolling arcade shooter: THE GLITTERATI,
disco raiders of 1978 who never left the dance floor. Their colours: mirror silver, gold,
white, with touches of teal and warm orange. {above} {down}
Top row, left to right: 1. the grunt: a mirror-ball drone, a small round silver disco ball
of square facets with two stubby gold fins at the top and one teal lens at the bottom.
2. the flanker: a gold medallion, a flat round gold disc with a star stamped on it, hung in
the middle of a wide swept-back crescent wing like a boomerang, a gold chain along the wing.
3. the tank, the largest and widest: a platform-boot stomper, two huge white platform boots
side by side joined by silver armour plates and rivets, their thick striped soles and
square toes pointing down, wide and heavy.
Middle row, left to right: 4. the diver: a dart, a narrow sharp arrowhead made of a huge
pointed white shirt collar, its two long lapel points swept back and up, a gold tip pointing
down. 5. the sniper: a small round silver pod with one very long thin spotlight barrel
pointing straight down, far longer than the pod, a teal lens at its end. 6. the splitter:
visibly two halves, a saucer made of one silver half and one gold half bolted together
along a dark seam down the middle, a pair of roller-skate wheels on each half.
Bottom row, left to right: 7. the mite, the smallest, tiny: one glittering silver facet
with four short points, a spark. 8. the captor, large: a silver saucer on roller-skate
wheels carrying one big gold gramophone horn whose wide open mouth points straight down.
9. the raider: a small gold saucer with two long jointed chrome arms reaching down and
forward, each ending in an open white-gloved hand.
