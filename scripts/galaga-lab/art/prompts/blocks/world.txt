The world: a space future in which everything stayed analog 1980s technology, drawn in the
comic-book manner of the big-sprite arcade boards of 1996 to 1998. Riveted, finned rockets;
wood-grain and bakelite; toggle switches, needle gauges, nixie and split-flap counters, cathode
tubes, tape reels, vacuum valves that glow orange, CB aerials, chrome trim, shag. Nothing
digital, no holograms, no touch screens, no sleek white science fiction. An 80s action cartoon
that knows it is silly: friendly, funny, nothing gory.
