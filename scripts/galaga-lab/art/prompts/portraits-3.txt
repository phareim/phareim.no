Three speakers, one to a row, the same head in all three squares of the row.
Top row, HANS DUO, a rival pizza delivery pilot who is sure of himself and wrong: a man of

brown leather flying jacket with a wool collar over a striped shirt, a cocky look. Left: a

edges; a few square flecks of mirror-ball light on the blue behind him. Left: calm, the
visor dark, the head tilted back. Middle: angry, the visor slit glowing red, the plume
bristling. Right: calm again, chin down.

riveted chrome face plate with two dark slanted eye slits and a grille of vertical slots
where a mouth would be, bolted over a bald head on a bull neck with huge bare shoulders,
leather straps and a spiked iron collar. Left: calm, the eye slits dark. Middle: angry, the
eye slits glowing orange, the head thrust forward, steam from the grille drawn as flat white
shapes. Right: calm again, head turned a little further.
