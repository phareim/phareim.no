{world}
The subject is the right side of the dashboard of a pizza delivery rocket: the instrument
panel. Brushed blue-grey steel plates with rivets, dark brown bakelite bezels, brass
frames, wood-grain trim, chrome.
{window}
From the top: one long narrow magenta window in a brass frame: the opening of a mechanical
counter. Under it a wide magenta window in a dark bakelite frame: an empty rack for a row
of valves, with five small brass sockets drawn on the frame under it. Under that, side by
side: at the left a square magenta window inside a round chrome dial ring with tick marks
round it; at the right a VU meter (the cream block): a cream-yellow meter face in a black
bakelite housing, with a curved black scale of small tick marks along the top of the face,
the last part of the scale red, and NO needle and no numbers on it: the face is left free
for a needle that is added later. Under those, one long narrow magenta window in a steel
frame: an empty row for small lamps. Under that, a wide magenta window in a brass frame
with two small chrome rollers at its ends: the opening of a paper strip-chart recorder.
Below the windows: a strip of wood-grain veneer with a row of four chrome toggle switches
and one small round needle gauge with a cream face; then plain riveted steel with a worn
round pizza-shop sticker showing a slice of pepperoni pizza (no words) at the left and a
louvred vent at the right, a few screws, a strip of chrome trim.
