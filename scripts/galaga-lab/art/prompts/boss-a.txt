The upper subject: SATURDAY KNIGHT, the boss of the disco raiders. A knight in a full suit of
gleaming white plate armour cut like a 1970s white three-piece suit: wide pointed armoured
lapels, a white breastplate over a dark shirt plate, flared armoured hips, a closed white
helmet with a dark visor slit and a small gold plume, no face showing. He stands facing the
viewer in a dance pose: one arm (the grey-white block at the left) raised straight up with
the finger pointing at the sky, the other hand on his hip. Directly above his head, as wide
as his shoulders, hangs a huge silver mirror ball of square facets (the chrome block) on a
short chain. White, silver and gold, with blue-violet shadows on the white. Not symmetrical:
the raised arm is on one side only.
The lower subject: DARK FADER, the boss of the Empire of Noise. The head and shoulders of a
towering figure in a wide flared helmet of dark slate blue-grey with pale grey edge lights
(dark, but never black), seen head-on. Where a face would be, the helmet has one vertical
slot down its middle with a silver mixing-desk fader knob sitting in it, and a small round
grille at each side of the slot. Under the helmet, very wide armoured shoulders and a cape,
and a chest panel with two round needle meters with yellow faces and a row of red and green
lamps. No purple.
