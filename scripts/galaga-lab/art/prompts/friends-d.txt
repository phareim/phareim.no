pixels wide and forty-six tall. {above} Each has its nose pointing straight UP. Seven

collar of cream-white fur round its nose and fur along its fins, a round blue bubble window,

olive-green steel with yellow-and-dark hazard stripes across it and on its bumpers, a winch

window and two slender wings; its whole right side is scorched brown and black with a torn
wing, its left side is clean. 4. a battered round grey saucer-freighter of patched plates
with rust-brown patches, a small round cockpit pod stuck out at its lower right, two short
striped prongs at the bottom, a small dish aerial at the upper right, and a flat white pizza
box with a red band strapped to the middle of its roof. 5, 6 and 7, much smaller and
pointing DOWN: a small rust-brown three-wheeled scrap cart, seen from above, with one very
long thin grey gun barrel pointing straight down from it, far longer than the cart.
