They are the two player ships of a 1997 vertical-scrolling arcade shooter, small: about
thirty-two pixels wide and thirty-six tall. {above} Each has its nose pointing straight UP
and its engine bells at the bottom. Six columns, every column the same ship all the way
down. The columns, from left to right: 1. THE WAGON, flying straight: a stubby, fat white
delivery rocket with a red nose cone, panels of brown wood grain along both flanks, a dark
blue windscreen behind the nose, two short red fins, twin engine bells, and on its roof a
rack with a strapped stack of flat tan pizza boxes. Exactly symmetrical from left to right.
2. THE SCOOTER, flying straight: a lean narrow red dart of a rocket, a round yellow headlamp
at the tip of the nose, a small yellow pizza slice painted behind it, a small dark blue
cockpit bubble, two swept-back red fins with white tips, one engine bell. Exactly
symmetrical. 3. the same WAGON rolled to its left, as a plane banks, still seen from
straight above with its nose straight up: the left half of the ship narrow and in shadow
with a short dark left fin, the right half wide and lit with a long bright right fin. 4. the
same SCOOTER rolled to its left in the same way: left fin short and dark, right fin long and
bright. 5. the Wagon flying straight again. 6. the Scooter flying straight again.
