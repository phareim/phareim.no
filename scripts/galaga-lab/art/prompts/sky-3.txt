They are far backdrop pieces that drift by behind the action of a vertical-scrolling space
shooter, and they must never be mistaken for anything in play: dark and low in contrast,
drawn in dark brown, deep rust, dusky plum-brown and night blue with one muted accent, no
bright highlights, no white, no saturated colour, never pure black. Large simple shapes
with few details, three or four close tones to a material.
Sector three, the Shag Nebula. The upper subject: a planet that is a lava lamp: a round
globe of dark amber-brown glass with four or five big slow blobs of dull rust-orange wax
floating inside it, a dark metal cap at its top pole and a dark metal base at its bottom
pole, like the two ends of a lava lamp.
The lower subject, wide: an op-art ring nebula: a great flattened ring, an ellipse seen at
an angle with nothing in its middle (the flat background shows through the hole), made of
concentric stripes by turns dark brown and dusky night blue, like an op-art target.
