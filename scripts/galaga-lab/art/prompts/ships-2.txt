They are the two player ships of a 1997 vertical-scrolling arcade shooter, each drawn twice,
small: about thirty large pixel blocks wide. {above} Each has its nose pointing straight UP
and its engine bells at the bottom. Keep each exactly as short and as wide as it is in the
reference: do not make them taller.
Top left: THE WAGON, flying straight: a stubby, fat white delivery rocket with a red nose
cone, panels of brown wood grain along both flanks, a dark windscreen behind the nose, two
short red fins, twin engine bells, and on its roof a rack with a strapped stack of flat tan
pizza boxes. Exactly symmetrical from left to right.
Top right: THE SCOOTER, flying straight: a lean narrow red dart of a rocket, a round yellow
headlamp at the tip of the nose, a small yellow pizza slice painted behind it, a small dark
blue cockpit bubble, two swept-back red fins with white tips, one engine bell. Exactly
symmetrical from left to right.
Bottom left: the same WAGON rolled to its left, as a plane banks, still seen from straight
above with its nose straight up and its body upright in the picture, not turned: the left
half of the ship is narrow and in shadow, with a short dark left fin; the right half is wide
and lit, with a long bright right fin; the roof rack sits left of the middle.
Bottom right: the same SCOOTER rolled to its left in the same way: nose straight up, body
upright, left fin short and dark, right fin long and bright.
The reds are warm brick red and scarlet, never pink. No pilots visible, no flames.
