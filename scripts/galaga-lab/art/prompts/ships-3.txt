They are the two player ships of a 1997 vertical-scrolling arcade shooter, each drawn twice,
small: about thirty pixel blocks wide, so every detail is a few blocks and the shapes are
bold. {above} Each has its nose pointing straight UP and its engine bells at the bottom.
Keep each exactly as small as it is in the reference: do not enlarge them, do not spread
them out to fill the picture; most of the picture stays empty magenta.
From left to right: 1. THE WAGON, flying straight: a stubby, fat white delivery rocket with
a red nose cone, panels of brown wood grain along both flanks, a dark windscreen behind the
nose, two short red fins, twin engine bells, and on its roof a rack with a strapped stack of
flat tan pizza boxes. Exactly symmetrical from left to right. 2. THE SCOOTER, flying
straight: a lean narrow red dart of a rocket, a round yellow headlamp at the tip of the
nose, a small yellow pizza slice painted behind it, a small dark blue cockpit bubble, two
swept-back red fins with white tips, one engine bell. Exactly symmetrical. 3. the same WAGON
rolled to its left, as a plane banks, still seen from straight above with its nose straight
up: the left half of the ship narrow and in shadow with a short dark left fin, the right
half wide and lit with a long bright right fin. 4. the same SCOOTER rolled to its left in
the same way: left fin short and dark, right fin long and bright.
The reds are warm brick red and scarlet, never pink. No pilots visible, no flames.
