They are one gang of enemies in a 1997 vertical-scrolling arcade shooter: THE EMPIRE OF
NOISE, the stage crew of an evil sound system. Their colours: charcoal and slate blue-grey
with pale grey edges so that they show against a dark sky, off-white armour, signal red,
and the green and yellow of meter lights. No purple and no violet anywhere. {above} {down}
Top row, left to right: 1. the grunt: a bow-tie fighter, which is simply a big bow tie: two
flat triangular wings of slate blue-grey cloth with pale edges, joined at a round knot in
the middle with a small red window. 2. the flanker: a static trooper, a rounded off-white
armoured helmet pod whose wide visor is a screen of black-and-white television static, a
short fin on each side. 3. the tank, the largest and widest: a flying mixing desk, a wide
slab of a sound console with rows of knobs and sliding faders and two round meters, armour
plates along its edges, wide and heavy.
Middle row, left to right: 4. the diver: a dart, one long chrome jack plug pointing straight
down, its tip with two dark rings, a short black cable stub at the top. 5. the sniper: a
small dark pod with one very long thin slotted shotgun-microphone barrel pointing straight
down, far longer than the pod. 6. the splitter: visibly two halves, a pair of big
headphones seen flat: two round padded ear cups side by side, joined only by a thin curved
head band.
Bottom row, left to right: 7. the mite, the smallest, tiny: one round dark knob with a
white pointer line. 8. the captor, large: a dark pod carrying one big round off-white
parabolic dish with a pickup on three thin struts in its middle, the dish facing down.
9. the raider: a fader drone, a square grey fader cap between two short rails, with two
long cable arms reaching down, each ending in a red crocodile clip.
