Three bosses, one to a row, the same head in all three squares of the row.
Top row, DOCTOR DURANT DURANT, who plays an organ the size of a church: a thin, vain man of
fifty with a long face, a pointed dark beard, slicked-back black hair with a grey streak,
arched eyebrows, a high stiff collar and a mustard-yellow robe. Left: calm, a thin superior
smile, eyes half closed. Middle: angry, teeth bared, eyes wide, one eyebrow twitching up.
Right: calm again, stroking his beard.
Middle row, CAL 9000, a machine that is a wall: there is no face, the portrait is a close
view of the machine straight on: one big round glass lens glowing deep red with a small
yellow point in its centre, set in a wide chrome ring in a panel of brushed blue-grey
steel, with two glowing orange vacuum valves at each side of it and rivets. This one row
is seen straight from the front, not three-quarter. Left: calm, the lens a steady deep red.
Middle: angry, the lens bright orange-red and larger, with a white-hot centre, all the
valves flaring yellow. Right: calm again.
Bottom row, DARK FADER, the boss of the Empire of Noise: no face, only his helmet: a wide
flared helmet of dark slate blue-grey with pale grey edge lights (dark, never black, no
purple), with one vertical slot down the middle of its face in which a silver mixing-desk
fader knob sits, and a small round grille at each side of the slot; armoured shoulders
below. Left: calm, the fader knob at the middle of its slot. Middle: angry, the fader knob
pushed to the very top of its slot, two small red lamps lit beside it. Right: calm again,
the fader low.
