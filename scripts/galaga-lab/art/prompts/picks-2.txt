They are the power-ups that fall down the screen in a 1997 arcade shooter: tiny icons,
sixteen large pixel blocks across each, so each is drawn with very few blocks, as its
boldest shape only, with a dark outline, lit from the upper left. Top row, left to right: a
glass vacuum tube with an orange filament on a brown base; a red and yellow toy ray gun; a
silver LaserDisc with a rainbow wedge; a dark grey boom box with two round speakers and a
handle; a slice of pepperoni pizza. Middle row: a blue soap-bubble wand with a pale bubble
in its ring; a black CB radio handset with a coiled cord; a silver mirror ball; a black VHS
cassette with two reel windows and a white label; a coral-red can of hairspray with a white
cap. Bottom row: a red horseshoe magnet with silver tips; a silver coin with a ridged rim
and no face on it; a larger vacuum valve on a brass socket, LIT, glowing orange and yellow
inside; and exactly the same valve UNLIT: the same outline, block for block, its glass dark
blue-grey and empty, the filament cold grey.
