They are the two player ships of a 1997 vertical-scrolling arcade shooter, each drawn twice.
{above} Each has its nose pointing straight UP the picture and its engine bells at the
bottom, and is symmetrical from left to right apart from what is strapped to it.
Top left: THE WAGON, flying straight. A stubby, fat delivery rocket, the station wagon of
rockets: a short rounded white hull with a red nose cone and a red stripe, panels of brown
wood grain along both flanks, chrome trim, a dark windscreen behind the nose, two short red
fins at the back, twin engine bells. On its roof a chrome roof rack carrying a strapped stack
of flat square pizza boxes (tan cardboard with a red band). Rivets along the seams.
Top right: THE SCOOTER, flying straight. A lean, narrow red dart of a rocket, a motor scooter
among rockets: a long thin red hull, a round yellow headlamp at the very tip of the nose, a
small yellow triangle of pizza slice painted behind the headlamp, a small dark cockpit bubble
in the middle, two swept-back red fins at the back with white tips, one engine bell, a chrome
exhaust pipe along one side.
Bottom left: the same WAGON, exactly the same ship at the same size, BANKING to its left: it
has rolled about its long axis so that its left side dips away from the viewer and its right
side rises towards the viewer. The left fin is short, narrow and in shadow; the right fin is
long, wide and lit; the roof rack sits a little left of the middle and more of the right
flank's wood panel shows. Nose still straight up.
Bottom right: the same SCOOTER, exactly the same ship at the same size, BANKING to its left
in the same way: left fin short, narrow and dark, right fin long, wide and lit, the cockpit
bubble a little left of the middle. Nose still straight up.
The reds are warm brick red and scarlet, never pink. No pilots visible, no flames.
